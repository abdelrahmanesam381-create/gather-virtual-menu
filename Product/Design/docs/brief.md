# Design brief

Gather is the working product name. The dashboard is a restaurant workbench: left navigation, a clear menu toolbar, ordered category sections and compact item rows. The public menu has a wide food banner, centered restaurant identity and generous item imagery. Use sage #426449, parchment #F8F9F5, white #FFFFFF, ink #223027, muted #637065 and border #DFE5DD. Use Georgia for restaurant display titles and system sans-serif for controls.

Desktop: 230 px sidebar / flexible content. Mobile: top brand and horizontal navigation; controls wrap without horizontal scrolling. All icons have accessible labels. Display order uses drag handles and explicit move buttons. Avoid decorative metric cards: this MVP is menu editing, not analytics.
